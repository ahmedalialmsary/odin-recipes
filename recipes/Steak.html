Steak

The easiest and best way to make steak is cooking it sous vide

ingredients

  • 2 rib-eye steaks, 1 1/2 inches thick, or another steak of your choice
  • Kosher salt
  • Fresh ground pepper
  • Additional spice rub of your choice
  • 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil

steps

  1. Season the steakSprinkle salt, black pepper, and a little garlic powder on both sides.
  2. Cook the steakHeat a pan with a little oil. Cook the steak for 3–5 minutes on each side (depending on how you like it).
  3. Rest and serveLet the steak rest for 5 minutes, then serve it with vegetables or mashed potatoes.
